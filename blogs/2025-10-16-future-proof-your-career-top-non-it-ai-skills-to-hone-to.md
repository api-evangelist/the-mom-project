In today’s job market, knowing about AI isn’t enough—you need to show you can work with it. Whether you're in tech, marketing, finance, or design, Generative AI is reshaping roles across many industries. A joint study by the UN labour agency (ILO) and Poland's National Research Institute reveals that Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI) impacts one in four jobs globally.
